## 1. Verdict

Flock can be built for Nigeria, Ghana and Kenya. None of the three markets has any rule about sheep, rams, wolves or fables (H, absence after a full read). The child-appeal rules that do exist are of two kinds:
- open "appeals to minors" tests: Lagos reg 7(1)(d), GCG Underage (i), Kenya Advertising reg 9(d);
- bans on **cartoons**: GCG Underage (ii), Kenya LN 112 reg 92(1)(d).

The approved art direction already answers both: life-like grown rams, muted dusk palette, no faces, no lambs.

Two findings are new to this game. First, the **ram is a religious and festival symbol in Nigeria** (Eid el-Kabir / Sallah), which brings in the Lagos ban on exploiting cultural traditions. Second, Lagos bans portraying gaming **in a context of toughness**. Both are answered by design and copy rules folded into the change.

Kenya remains blocked for this platform by data localisation (LN 112 reg 42(2)), as for every Triptown game.

## 3. What already passes (design intent, inherited behaviour verified on Beat the Gate)

- **No new maths.** Skin on `whack-crash`, verified by `apps/api/src/flock-registration.test.ts` (3 tests pass, 4 Oct 2026) and a clean `packages/fairness/reports/`.
- **No advance warning.** The wolf appears only on `onCrash`. The flock size is `flockCount(multiplier)` and pace comes from elapsed time; neither sees the crash time (spec; `GameStage` receives only the multiplier, `packages/crash-client/src/game/screen.ts:143-154`).
- **No near miss or harm.** The pull-up starts only after settlement. The wolf stops short and takes no ram, and the flock bolts facing its direction of travel (spec).
- **Results.** No celebration at or below the stake. This is the shared `resultPresentation` decision, verified by `presentation-check` on Beat the Gate (`docs/compliance/evidence/2026-09-17/beat-the-gate-presentation-check.json`).
- **Cycle gap and release-to-press.** Shared controller, verified by `timing-check` under `ng-draft` on Beat the Gate.
- **Rules before betting**, plus the withholding notice. Shared `RulesPanel`, with Flock's catalogue in `apps/flock/src/i18n/en.ts`.
- **Classification.** One flock, no race, no runners to back: a casino crash game, not virtual racing (Lagos Law s.1 excludes virtual horse racing from "horse racing"; Flock has no race at all).
