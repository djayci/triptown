## 1. Verdict

Gate Rush can be built for Nigeria and Ghana. Neither market publishes game-design rules that forbid a deferred reveal. The risks are procedural: both markets certify software through a lab, and the lab reads GLI-19-style rules ("not misleading", pay-out disclosure).

With exact live odds on screen, rules stating the result is fixed at round start, and no near-miss presentation, the design addresses those rules on paper. Written acceptance from Lagos's lab and the GCG is the gate before shipping.

Deferred reveal must stay off in Brazil and Ontario, and should stay off in the UK until reviewed.
